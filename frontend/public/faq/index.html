CheckAm questions and answers

Quick answers about checking products with CheckAm.

What is CheckAm?

CheckAm is a free web app that lets you look up food, drinks and medicines in the NAFDAC public registry by name or NAFDAC number, or by taking a photo of the pack.

Is CheckAm official or run by NAFDAC?

No. CheckAm is independent and is not affiliated with NAFDAC. It shows what the NAFDAC public registry says and links you to take the next step yourself.

Does CheckAm tell me if a product is fake?

No. CheckAm reports what the registry shows. It does not decide whether a product is genuine, so always inspect the pack and contact NAFDAC for enforcement.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The registry list is saved on your phone, so searching and checking works offline. Some extras, such as the optional AI help, need a connection.

Is it free?

Yes. CheckAm is free to use and there is nothing to install: open it in your browser and, if you like, add it to your home screen.

Where do I find the NAFDAC number on a pack?

Look for the words NAFDAC Reg. No. near the ingredients, barcode or side panel. It is usually a prefix and digits, such as A4-2260 or 01-4721.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The photo of the pack is read on your phone and is not uploaded. If you use the optional AI help, only the text you typed is sent.

What does the optional AI help do?

When a product is not in the registry, you can ask AI for a summary of what the genuine pack looks like. It can make mistakes and is not from NAFDAC, and a small number of lookups are available each day.

Which products can I check?

Food, drinks and medicines in the NAFDAC registry, with some care products too. The list is updated as the registry changes.
