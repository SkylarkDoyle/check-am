What to check on a pack before you buy

A few quick checks take less than a minute and tell you a lot. Use them together with the NAFDAC registry result.

On the pack

  • NAFDAC number: is there one, and is it printed clearly?
  • Name and maker: do they match what the registry shows for that number?
  • Dates: is there a manufacture or expiry date, and is it still in date?
  • Print quality: look for blurry print, spelling mistakes or colours that look different from the usual pack.
  • Seal and wrapper: torn, loose or re-stuck seals are a reason to look twice.
  • Size and details: does the pack size and address look right for the brand?

Before you pay

  • Check the number in CheckAm and compare the result with the pack.
  • Buy from shops and sellers you trust, and keep your receipt.
  • If something does not match, do not buy it, and consider reporting it to NAFDAC.

What a registry check can and cannot tell you

A registry match means the number exists in the registry and shows the product details on file. It does not prove that the pack in your hand is genuine. That is why the checklist above matters too.
