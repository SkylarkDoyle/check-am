How to check a NAFDAC number

Every NAFDAC-registered food, drink and medicine should carry a registration number on its pack. Here is how to find it and look it up before you buy.

1. Find the number on the pack

Look near the ingredients, the barcode or the side panel for the words "NAFDAC Reg. No." or "NAFDAC No." followed by a short code. It is usually a prefix and digits, for example A4-2260 or 01-4721. If you cannot find any number, that is worth noting before you buy.

2. Type it in, or take a photo

In CheckAm, type the number or the product name. Or tap the scan button, take a photo of the pack, drag the box over the number and let the app read it. If a character is misread, you can fix it before checking, and the app suggests close matches.

3. Read the answer

You will see what the NAFDAC registry shows for that number: the product, the maker and the registration status. Then compare it with the pack in your hand: the name, the maker and the number should agree.

4. Use your own judgement

A registry entry is a helpful check, not a guarantee. Look at the packaging, the seals and the expiry date, buy from places you trust, and report concerns to NAFDAC directly.
